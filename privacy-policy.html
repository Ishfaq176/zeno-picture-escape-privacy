Privacy Policy for Zeno Picture Escape

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Zeno Picture Escape is an Android game developed and published by Zeno Network Labs (Android package com.zenonetwork.zenopictureescape). This policy explains the information used by the game and its account, advertising and purchase services.

You can play the offline campaign without creating an account. Google sign-in is optional for ordinary offline play and is used for account-based Premium features and optional cloud progress.

Account or data deletion: Open the account-deletion request page. You can request deletion without installing or signing in to the game.

1. Information used by the game

  • Local gameplay: level progress, stars, settings, controls, cosmetic choices, challenge records, checkpoints and pending item-recovery records are stored in the app's local storage.
  • Optional Google account: Google sign-in and Firebase Authentication process your account identifier, verified email address, display name and profile image information. The game does not receive your Google password.
  • Optional cloud progress: if you enable backup or request a backup/restore, Firebase Firestore stores or merges campaign progress such as unlocked stages, best stars and earned collection/cosmetic records for your signed-in account. Ordinary local progress is not automatically uploaded while cloud backup is off.
  • Premium and purchases: Google Play processes payments. The game and its Firebase backend process product and order identifiers, purchase tokens for verification, purchase status, account binding, entitlement and inventory counts, item-operation receipts, reward claims and refund/reconciliation records. Stored purchase-verification ledgers use token hashes; purchase tokens are processed when verifying with Google. Zeno Network Labs does not receive your full payment-card or bank details from Google Play.
  • Advertising and technical data: Google advertising and consent services may process advertising/device identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, app/device information, ad interactions, diagnostics and consent choices. Firebase, Google Play and App Check/Play Integrity may process app/device integrity signals, security tokens and technical request or diagnostic information to operate and protect their services.
  • Support requests: when you contact us, we receive your email address, message and the account details you choose to provide for support or ownership verification.

2. How information is used and shared

Information is used to run the game, save and restore progress, authenticate the account you choose, verify purchases, deliver Premium inventory and rewards, recover interrupted item requests, reconcile refunds, prevent fraud or duplicate claims, provide support and show ads to eligible free players.

The relevant service providers are Google Sign-In/Credential Manager, Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Cloud Functions, Firebase App Check/Google Play Integrity, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Information required for those services is processed by Google and its infrastructure under their applicable terms. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect the service. We do not sell your personal account information.

See Google's Privacy Policy, Firebase Privacy and Security and Google's use of information from partner apps.

3. Advertising and your choices

Free players may see a dashboard banner, interstitials at level transitions and optional rewarded ads for in-game benefits. Gameplay does not contain a banner. Verified Premium access removes in-app ads. Where applicable, UMP handles advertising consent and privacy options; the game's Settings includes Privacy Choices. You can decline optional rewarded ads and manage advertising controls offered by Google and Android.

4. Storage, security and retention

Local records remain until removed through an available reset, Android's app-storage controls or uninstalling the game. Signing out or uninstalling does not itself delete an existing server account or cloud records.

Account, cloud-progress and active Premium records are kept to provide the services you use and are reviewed for deletion when you request account deletion. Limited purchase, transaction, security and fraud-prevention records may be retained where necessary for accounting, verification, abuse prevention or legal obligations. They are not an active game profile and are not used to keep a deleted account's access active. Retention depends on the purpose and applicable obligations; when processing a request, we explain any data that must be retained and why. Google may retain its own payment, advertising or technical records under its policies.

Configured network services use encrypted connections, Firebase account controls and server-side purchase checks. These measures do not guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process data outside your country.

5. Account and associated data deletion

To request deletion, visit Request Account Deletion or email info@zenonetwork.xyz with the subject Zeno Picture Escape Account Deletion. Include the Google email used inside this game and your Firebase UID if you know it. We may request reasonable confirmation that you control the account. Never send a password, one-time code or payment-card details.

After ownership verification, the request covers the game's Firebase sign-in account, associated profile/account data, cloud progress and active Premium entitlement/inventory records, subject to the limited retention described above. This is a support-handled request process; opening the page or composing an email does not automatically delete data. You do not need to reinstall the app to submit a request.

Deleting this game account does not delete your Google account, clear local game data on your device or issue a Google Play refund. Removing account access may remove Premium and cloud-restoration access. Refund requests are handled through Google Play. To remove only local game data, use Android Settings → Apps → Zeno Picture Escape → Storage → Clear storage/data, or uninstall.

6. Parents and guardians

If you are a parent or guardian with a question about information associated with a child's use of the game, contact the support address below to request review or deletion. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information in your first message.
