Privacy Policy for Zeno Picture Escape

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Zeno Picture Escape is developed and published by Zeno Network Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Zeno Picture Escape mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

Zeno Picture Escape does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other direct personal information to play the game.

Game progress, unlocked levels, stars, settings, and similar gameplay preferences are stored locally on your device unless otherwise stated in a future version.

2. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display ads, including rewarded ads and interstitial ads. AdMob may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, app interaction data, diagnostic information, and other data as described by Google's policies.

Rewarded ads are optional and may be used to provide in-game benefits such as an extra life or recharging selected power-ups. Interstitial ads may be shown at natural transition points between levels.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify or measure devices and app activity. The main third-party service currently used is:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK

You can review Google's privacy practices at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children's Privacy

Zeno Picture Escape is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If applicable laws require parental consent or age-based restrictions for advertising or data processing, we aim to use available platform and advertising controls to comply with those requirements.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to reduce unnecessary collection of user information. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Locally stored game progress remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Data handled by Google AdMob is subject to Google's own retention and privacy practices.

7. Your Choices

You may choose not to watch optional rewarded ads. Depending on your device, region, and Google settings, you may also have controls related to personalized advertising and advertising identifiers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, advertising setup, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The updated date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Zeno Picture Escape, contact Zeno Network Labs through the developer contact information shown on the app's Google Play Store listing.
