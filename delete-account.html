Delete your Zeno Picture Escape account

Zeno Network Labs · Last updated: 31 October 2026

This page is for the Android game Zeno Picture Escape (com.zenonetwork.zenopictureescape). You can request deletion of your game account and associated data without installing or signing in to the app.

Send your deletion request

Email an account-deletion request

The button opens your email application with a draft addressed to info@zenonetwork.xyz. If no email app opens, use any webmail or email service to send to that address.

  • Subject: Zeno Picture Escape Account Deletion
  • Include: the Google email you used to sign in inside this game, and your Firebase UID if known. A UID is optional.
  • State: that you want your Zeno Picture Escape account and associated data deleted.

Never send your Google password, OTP/verification code, full payment-card number or bank credentials. We may ask you to confirm control of the account before processing its deletion.

What happens next

Zeno Network Labs handles requests through support, verifies ownership where needed and processes deletion of the game's Firebase sign-in account, associated profile/account data, cloud progress and active Premium entitlement/inventory records. Opening this page or preparing an email does not automatically delete anything. We will explain any ownership check, request status and data that must be retained.

Data that may be retained

Limited purchase/transaction verification, item-operation, refund, security and fraud-prevention records may be retained where necessary for accounting, duplicate-claim prevention, legal compliance or service security. Retained records are not an active game profile and do not keep a deleted account's game access active. Retention depends on the applicable purpose and obligations. We explain the reason for any necessary retention when handling your request. Google Play and other Google services control their own records under their policies. See the Privacy Policy.

Local data, Google accounts and refunds

  • Local data only: use Android Settings → Apps → Zeno Picture Escape → Storage → Clear storage/data, or uninstall. This resets local progress/settings but does not itself delete server records.
  • Server account: use the email request above. Deletion may remove Premium and cloud-restore access; it does not automatically clear local copies on your device.
  • Google account: deleting the game's account does not delete your Google account.
  • Refunds: account deletion is not a refund request. Use Google Play's purchase support/refund process for a Play payment.
